# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-04-29

- Initial Clan Cast release.
- Create and manage distribution lists from device contacts.
- Author reusable templates with supported personalization tokens.
- Compose, validate, and preview broadcasts before launch.
- Save broadcast snapshots and review detailed recipient history.
- Send broadcasts through a foreground SMS service with pause, resume, and cancel controls.
- Recover interrupted sends safely and provide clearer progress, completion, and deletion confirmations.
